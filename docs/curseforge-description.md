**Never run around begging for Fort again.**

Buff Digger shows you which players around you can buff you, and asks them for you in one click. When the buff lands, one more click thanks them. That's it.

---

## How it works

1. **Look:** an icon shows up next to every friendly player who can give you a buff you're missing. Fort, Kings, Might, Mark, Intellect.
2. **Click:** a polite whisper goes out, with the spell linked. Different wording every time, so you don't sound like a bot.
3. **Thank:** the buff lands, the line turns gold. Click it: /thank and a thank-you. Never on its own. Someone buffed you unasked? Their line turns gold too (one box in the settings to turn that off).

No commands, no macros. Everything is a click in a window.

---

## What you get

- **The right buff for your class.** Warriors get Might before Wisdom, casters get Wisdom and Intellect, locks get Fort first. Don't agree? Reorder it in the settings.
- **The best rank around.** That level 15 priest's Fort is weak: Buff Digger waits for the level 45 one running by.
- **Running out?** Five minutes before a buff drops, it asks again.
- **One whisper per player.** Fort and Spirit from the same priest? Ctrl+click both icons in Get buffs, hit the arrow: asked together.
- **Never nags.** Same player, same buff: it waits before asking again.
- **Pally, priest, mage or druid?** The Give buffs window lists who around lacks your buff. Click, they're buffed.
- **Two Buff Digger users?** No whisper at all: the buffer gets the request in a window and buffs you in one click.
- **Eight languages** for the messages you send: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Russian. Or write your own.
- **Out of combat only.** Everything hides when the pull starts.

---

## Getting started

Open the settings from the minimap button and pick a look for friendly nameplates: **Name only** keeps just their name, **Icons only** keeps just Buff Digger's icons. Buff Digger finds players through them: WoW gives addons no other way to see who's around. Then go somewhere busy.

---

## Good to know

- It says **"probably"** for a reason: in Forever, spells are bought at the trainer, and no addon can see another player's spellbook. Buff Digger goes by class and level.
- Made for the **open world and cities**. In dungeons, Blizzard locks friendly nameplates and addon messages.
- Bug or wrong number? Tell us in the comments.
